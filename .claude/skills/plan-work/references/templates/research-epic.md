# Research Epic Template
Labels: `epic` + the story's `area:*`. Under the story's milestone (or none). Children = lane issues.
```markdown
## Goal
Replace the assumptions in <story> with facts before its design is decided.

## Questions (one lane each)
1. #<lane> — <question>
2. …

## Rules
Findings live as comments on the lane issues; the composite lands here; nothing is committed. Owner sign-off is a hard stop before any design or implementation issue is filed.

## Status
<lanes dispatched / reported / composite posted / awaiting sign-off / ratified>
```
