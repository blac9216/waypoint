# Decision record template

Kept in scratch during the rounds; written to the location the caller named at the end.
One entry per question, in the order asked; sidebars nest under the question that raised
them. Never rewrite an earlier entry — append a correction.

```markdown
# <Topic> — interrogation record (<date>)

Ceremony: spike | bounded | architectural. Record location: <where this lives>.
Rounds: <R>. Questions: <N total>. Open items at close: none | <list>.

## Round 1 — <categories with counts>

### <ID> <title>
**Decision:** <the owner's answer, as a statement — not "(a)"; write what (a) meant>
**Context:** <clarifications, constraints or reasons the owner gave — verbatim where it matters>
**Follow-ups queued:** <IDs> | none

### <ID> <title> — sidebar
**Resolution:** <what was agreed after the discussion>
**Changes to queued questions:** <dropped/added> | none

## Round 2 — …

## Self-review
Placeholders: none. Contradictions: <none | resolved how>. Scope: <in bounds | what was
excluded>. Ambiguity: <none | which answers were re-confirmed>.

## Summary of decisions
- <one line per decision, grouped by category — the part the caller acts on>
```
